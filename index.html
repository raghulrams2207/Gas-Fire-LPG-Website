Safety Standards

Six-point Safety
Inspection Protocol

01
Pressure Hydrostatic Test

All cylinders are pressure-tested to 2× working pressure. Only passing cylinders are filled and dispatched.

02
Valve & Seal Integrity Check

Brass valves are ultrasonically checked for micro-leaks. Anti-tamper seals are applied at point of fill.

03
Certified Delivery Agents

All delivery personnel hold HPCL/BPCL safety certifications and carry proper identification.

04
Weight Verification at Delivery

Each cylinder is weighed in front of the customer. Short-fill cylinders are immediately replaced.
